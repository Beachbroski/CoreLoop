How it runs

Three steps. The money only moves at the third one, and you are the one who moves it.

1Post a brief

Say what you need, what you can spend and when you need it. It goes out to creators in your city inside the iPhone app. Posting is free and you can close it at any time.

2Read who applies

Creators apply with a pitch and the rate they want, so you are comparing real people and real numbers rather than a rate card. Accepting one authorizes that rate on your card.

3Approve, and only then pay

The creator delivers in the app. You approve, ask for a revision, or decline. Approving captures the hold: $400.00 off your card, $340.00 to Ana Reyes. Declining charges nothing.

Why not an agency

The difference is in the terms, not in a feature list, so here are the terms.

  CreatorDocks A local agency
Cost to start Nothing. Posting a brief is free, and it stays free if nobody is right for the job. A retainer or a minimum spend, agreed before any work starts.
Commitment One brief at a time. Post one in March, none in April, two in May. A three or six month contract, signed before the first shoot.
Who picks the creator You do. Creators apply with a pitch and their own rate, and you read every one of them. An account manager assigns somebody from their roster.
When money moves Held when you accept, charged when you approve. Decline and nothing is charged. Invoiced on a schedule, usually before you have seen the work.
If the content is wrong Ask for a revision and the hold stays in place, or decline and nothing is charged. A change request, another round of hours, and another line on the invoice.

Creators are on iPhone

The other half of this is an app. Creators browse the briefs posted near them, apply with the rate they want, deliver the files, and get paid through Stripe the moment you approve. Same artwork, same numbers, same words on both sides of the deal.

Before your first brief

What does it cost?
Nothing to post and nothing monthly. We take a 15% platform fee out of the rate you agreed, never on top of it. Accept at $400.00 and $400.00 is what leaves your card; $340.00 goes to the creator.
How do payments work?
Accepting a creator authorizes their rate on your card — held, not charged. When they deliver you approve, ask for a revision, or decline. Only approving captures the money and pays the creator. Payments run on Stripe.
How are creators vetted?
By hand. Creators apply, we read every profile before inviting them, and each one finishes Stripe payout onboarding — identity and bank details verified — before any brand can accept them.
When can I start?
Access is invite-led while we build up creators city by city, so there is no self-serve signup yet. Request access with your email and we will open your account and help you write the first brief.
