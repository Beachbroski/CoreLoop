Tokens and type

The specimen sheet for the CreatorDocks web redesign. Every colour figure below is computed from the WCAG relative-luminance formula against the ground that token is actually used on, not copied from the brief. Every cover-art cell is drawn by the same generator the iPhone app runs.

All 124 pairs on this sheet clear their minimum. The tightest is dark white on --cd-accent-fill, at 4.51 against a floor of 4.50. Nothing here has margin to spare, so no token may be nudged without re-running this sheet.

Sources: 01-design-brief.md §2–§5 and §8, mobile/src/constants/theme.ts, mobile/src/lib/cover-art.ts, lib/utils.ts. Generated by tools/build-tokens.mjs from tools/baked-specs.json.

What this sheet is asking you to approve

Seven decisions. Everything else on the canvas assumes these are settled.

The two typefaces and the scale
Archivo for display and data, Schibsted Grotesk for everything read. No Inter, no mono, no third family.
The radius philosophy
Rectangles, not pills. Buttons are 6px. Nothing lands between 18px and 40px.
Two new tokens
--cd-bg-raised, because four surface levels need four fills on dark; --cd-text-faint, because rules and disabled glyphs are not captions.
The --cd-text-faint demotion
It is rated as a non-text token at 3:1 and no sentence may be set in it. Every caption on the site moves up to --cd-text-secondary. This is a real change to existing pages.
Two alpha divergences from mobile
--cd-accent-soft drops from 0.16 to 0.10 on dark and 0.10 to 0.06 on light, because the web puts accent-coloured text on it and mobile only puts icons.
The money microcopy
The pair, the fee taken out of the rate, the number on the button, the minus sign that is not red.
The brand mark
Option B, the drawn slip, is on every other artboard; Option A, the set letter, is here as the candidate it beat. Keep B, or send the five page artboards back to be re-marked.

Colour

Two complete palettes. The four grounds are the four surface levels, and every foreground is measured on all of them, because a token that only works on the canvas is a token that breaks in a modal.

Light keeps the same two roles rather than collapsing them. --cd-accent is darkened until it can be read as a word on a light ground (5.22 on the canvas, where the dark theme's #ff503d manages only 3.03); --cd-accent-fill stays lighter so a coral button still reads as coral rather than brick (4.22 against the canvas it sits on) while white on it holds at 4.52. Why text on artwork always sits on a scrim

7 of the 8 lighter palette stops put white below 4.5:1, so white on artwork is never safe by inspection. --cd-art-scrim rgba(7,8,11,0.55) fixes it: over the worst of the 16 stops, #f59e0b, white reads 7.66. Initials discs use the darker stop instead, where white is never below 5.88. The price pill needs neither, because it is --cd-accent-fill carrying white at 4.51.

Elevation

Four levels, and a level implies its whole recipe. On dark, lightness carries the step and box-shadow appears only at level 3. On light, white caps the lightness so shadow carries it. One mechanism per theme.

Dark
0 canvas --cd-bg. No border, no shadow. This cell looks empty because it is: level 0 is the room the other three sit in.
1 surface --cd-bg-element + 1px --cd-border + --cd-elev-1 (none).
2 raised --cd-bg-raised + 1px --cd-border + --cd-elev-2, which on dark is a 1px inner top highlight, not a drop shadow. It is drawn here, where the token is the subject; the other artboards omit it, because the canvas rule for Phase 1 is no box-shadow on a dark surface below level 3 and at 4% alpha the highlight is invisible at artboard scale.
3 overlay --cd-bg-raised + 1px rgba(255,255,255,0.14) + --cd-elev-3, over a rgba(7,8,11,0.72) page scrim. The only level on dark that may carry a box-shadow.
Light
0 canvas --cd-bg. No border, no shadow. This cell looks empty because it is: level 0 is the room the other three sit in.
1 surface --cd-bg-element + 1px --cd-border.
2 raised --cd-bg-raised (= white) + --cd-elev-2. Light caps at white, so the step is a shadow. One mechanism per theme, never both.
3 overlay --cd-bg-raised + --cd-elev-3, over a rgba(15,23,42,0.32) scrim.

Money

The eight rules that govern every figure on the site, each shown with the fixture numbers the other artboards use.

Always show the pair.

Wherever a rate sits next to an action, show what the brand pays and what the creator nets. Never one without the other.

$400.00charged·$340.00to Ana Reyes
The fee is "out of", never "plus".

There is no arrangement in which the brand pays more than the accepted rate, so the copy never implies one.

15% platform fee, taken out of the rate. Never written: plus fees, +15%, excluding fees.
Money buttons carry the number.

The amount lives on the control that moves it, so nobody has to look away to check what they are agreeing to.
