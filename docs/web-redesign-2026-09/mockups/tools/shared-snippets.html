11  FAQ definition-list row

A <dl>, not cards. Question in h3, answer in body-lg at 68ch, 1px --cd-border between rows. Two columns at ≥1024. Marketing only, so dark only.

When is my card actually charged?
When you approve the content. Accepting a creator puts a hold on the card for the accepted rate; approving captures that hold. If you decline the work, nothing is charged.
What does the 15% come out of?
The rate you agreed, not a surcharge on top of it. Accept at $400.00 and $400.00 is what you pay; the creator receives $340.00.
